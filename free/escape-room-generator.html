How it works

Print the pages (black-and-white is fine). Lock a few treats in a box, tin or shoebox and tape the printed keypad on the lid — or set a real 3-digit combination padlock to the code. Hide the three clue cards around one room. Each clue reveals one digit; line the three digits up to crack the code and open the vault. Setup is about 5 minutes and play runs 10–15 minutes.

Is it really free?

Yes — no sign-up, no email, no watermark. Generate as many as you like.

Do I need a colour printer or a padlock?

Neither. It's designed for plain black-and-white paper, and you can tape the printed keypad on a box instead of using a real padlock.

What ages does it suit?

Ages 4–9. Pick the age band and the puzzles scale from simple counting up to adding and symbol-decoding.
